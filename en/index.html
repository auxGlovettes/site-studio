The studio

A 39 m² studio designed for families and small groups: a 160 × 200 cm double bed in the alcove, 80 × 190 cm bunk beds in the cabin corner, a balcony and a view over the forest.

Practical info

Check-in from 3 pm, check-out before 11 am. Self check-in with a key box: we remain reachable by phone throughout your stay.

Getting here

By car: free parking at the foot of the residence.
By coach: services from Grenoble to Villard-de-Lans, then a free shuttle to Les Glovettes (in season).

Self-catering

The studio is rented without linen and without cleaning. You can request these services when booking (see below).

Accessibility

There is a lift up to the studio, but a few steps to enter the building and one step to reach the balcony. Full wheelchair access is not possible. Any special requirements? Please contact us before booking.

Around Les Glovettes

The residence is at the foot of the slopes, in the heart of the Vercors Regional Nature Park. The centre of Villard-de-Lans is about 10 minutes away by car.

❄️ In winter

  • Chairlift from the residence: access to the Espace Villard-Corrençon ski area (about 125 km of slopes)
  • Beginner area for children
  • Snowshoeing: easy loop from the resort
  • Cross-country skiing at Bois Barbu
  • Equipment rental on site

☀️ In summer

  • Hiking and trail running from the residence
  • Road and mountain biking, climbing, paragliding
  • Games and sports pitches in the residence

🏔️ All year round

  • Swimming pool, ice rink and bowling in the village
  • Orienteering trails on local heritage and the Resistance
  • Free shuttles in season
